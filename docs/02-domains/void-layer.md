> **READ THIS FIRST — STRUCTURAL NOTE.** The Void is **not a domain.** It has no
> Stone, no government, no economy, no army, no homeland, no people, and no
> civilization. It is a **cross-domain corruption LAYER** — a metaphysical
> condition that can appear *inside any domain, any faction, any relic, any
> person, and any heroic act.* This document therefore **adapts** the Domain
> Bible Template: where the template asks for "government," "class structure,"
> "military doctrine," or "homeland," this Bible substitutes the Void's true
> equivalents — **vectors, faces, resonance stages, echoes, and the cells it
> grows inside other domains.** Every section that does not apply to a layer is
> explicitly reframed rather than faked. The Void does not *own* territory. It
> *infiltrates* everyone else's.

## 1. Identity Snapshot — The Void in One Minute

The Void is the thing that works.

That is the whole danger, compressed. It is not purple fog, not a demon, not
"dark magic," not a monster, and not the source of Abyss's evil. It is the
shortcut that delivers. You needed the spell to be stronger, and it was. You
needed the dying child to live, and she lived. You needed the battle won, the
relic empowered, the path revealed, the wall to hold one more hour — and every
one of those things happened, exactly as promised. The Void keeps its promises.
That is how it lies.

The Void is **three things at once, always:**

- **ENHANCEMENT** — it removes the natural limit, the check, the cost-at-the-door. Faster, stronger, cheaper, *at first.* It does not add power so much as it *strips the brake.*
- **PARASITE** — it feeds on instability. It takes the thing a domain already loves and makes it extreme, then lives in the excess. It does not invade what is whole; it nests in what is already cracking.
- **LIAR** — it never tells you to become evil. It tells you to become *effective.* It speaks partial truths, true enough to act on, incomplete enough to ruin you.

Secondary faces (all true simultaneously): **amplifier**, **identity solvent**,
**reality destabilizer**, **false shortcut**. It amplifies what you bring it. It
dissolves the boundaries of selfhood the way solvent dissolves glue. It loosens
the seams of reality where it is used heavily. And it is, finally, a road that
goes the right direction for exactly long enough to commit you to it.

**The locked truth a reader must leave this section with:** the Void is dangerous
**precisely because it is useful.** If it only ever failed, no one would touch it.
It is feared by the wise and adored by the desperate because *it gives real
benefit and then extracts a hidden, never-clean cost* — identity damage,
soul-flow disruption, memory distortion, emotional dependency, reality
instability, Stone stress, spiritual parasitism, or future Keeper pressure. Often
several at once. Always paid later.

## 2. Hard Canon

These are locked from [Chunk 1 §3](../01-foundation/chunk-01-master-canon-foundation.md)
and may be **expanded but never contradicted.**

1. **The Void is enhancement, parasite, and liar** — also amplifier, identity solvent, reality destabilizer, and false shortcut. (Locked Decision #8, Chunk 1.)
2. **The Void is NOT "evil magic"** and **NOT "purple corruption fog."** (CANON-WARDEN, Chunk 1.) It must stay useful, seductive, parasitic, and identity-damaging.
3. **The Void is NOT the origin of Abyssian evil.** Abyss is coherently evil because its root is the Keeper. The Void made Abyss *more powerful, more unstable, and more capable of damaging reality* — not evil in the first place. (Locked Decision #9, Chunk 1.)
4. **The Void gives real results.** A stronger spell, a saved life, a won battle, a revealed path, an empowered relic, a temporary stabilization — these are genuine, not illusions. (Chunk 1 §3; Chunk 4 Locked Decision #19: "The Void gives real benefits but never clean power.")
5. **The cost is never clean.** Identity damage, soul-flow disruption, memory distortion, emotional dependency, reality instability, Stone stress, spiritual parasitism, or future Keeper pressure — usually paid later, often paid by someone other than the user.
6. **As enhancement, the Void hollows meaning.** Void-amplified Radiance flame burns hotter *without the inner cost*, teaching that spectacle can replace devotion.
7. **As parasite, the Void feeds on instability** — broken identity, contradictory memory, interrupted soul flow, violated oaths, love-become-control, virtue-become-obsession.
8. **As liar, the Void tells partial truths.** It rarely says "become evil." It says "become effective."
9. **The Void can threaten even Abyss.** Abyss serves the Keeper's *order*; the Void offers dissolution *beneath* order. The Void is loyal to no one, including the Keeper's instrument.
10. **A heroic action can create metaphysical damage if performed through Void methods.** (Chunk 1 §15.)
11. **Void temptation must always sound plausible.** "You saved them. That is all that matters." (Chunk 3; Chunk 4.)

## 3. Metaphysical Role — Where the Void Sits in Reality

The world is held together by stabilizing systems: the **Architect Sacrifice**,
the **fractured Nexus Stone** resonating through the five domain Stones, **soul
flow**, **memory continuity**, **identity coherence**, **death-passage law**, and
**reality-bound oaths**. Together these form the **prison outside reality** that
holds the Keeper. The prison **weakens when these systems weaken** (Chunk 1 §2.5).

The Void's metaphysical role is to be **the universal solvent of those exact
systems** — but it does not attack them like an army. It offers to *help you with
your immediate problem* using power drawn from loosening them. Every act of Void
enhancement spends a little of the lattice that holds the Keeper, and routes the
proceeds to you as benefit-now.

**Relationship to the Keeper.** The Keeper is the imprisoned evil Architect; the
Void is the medium through which the Keeper's *strategy* (Chunk 1 §2.3.8: "Use
the Void as enhancement, parasite, and liar") becomes available to people who have
never heard of the Keeper. Crucially: **the Void is not simply "the Keeper's
magic."** The Keeper *uses* the Void, but the Void's parasitism would still hollow
a person even if the Keeper had no plan for them. The Keeper benefits from the
Void the way a warlord benefits from a flood he did not cause but knows how to
exploit. (Whether the Keeper *made* the flood is the Source Question, §4.)

**Relationship to the prison.** Every Void enhancement is, at the metaphysical
level, a small **withdrawal against the prison's structural integrity.** The user
feels it as free power. The lattice feels it as a thinning seam. This is why
Void-amplified *heroism* still raises `keeper_prison_pressure`: the prison does
not care about your intentions, only about how much of itself you spent.

**Relationship to soul flow.** Healthy soul flow lets the dead pass, memory stay
coherent, and identity stay stable (Chunk 1 §2.7). The Void's enhancement
typically routes power *through* soul flow — borrowing against death passage,
memory, or identity to fund the effect. So Void use tends to leave **soul-flow
wounds** of type `void_amplification`, and adjacent wounds (`memory_fracture`,
`identity_dissolution`) as the parasite settles in.

**Relationship to reality stability.** Heavy or repeated Void use **destabilizes
reality locally** before it does so cosmically. This is the engine of **Void
Echoes** (§8): where reality is thin, false memories, mimic-voices, echo-NPCs,
and false paths bleed through, and `reality_stability` drops.

**One-line role:** *The Void is the price-hiding interface between a person's
urgent need and the dissolution of the systems that imprison the Keeper.*

## 4. The Source Question — Competing Theories

> **Open by canon (Chunk 1 §16):** Is the Void older than the Keeper, discovered
> by it, or produced by the prison boundary? Can it be purified or only resisted/
> contained? Does it have will, or mimic will through parasitic patterning?

This Bible **resolves some and deliberately leaves some open**, so that VERDAX can
present any of them as in-world "truth" depending on who is speaking, and so the
campaign can reveal an answer at `keeper_revelation` phase without contradicting
earlier lore.

### Theory A — The Older Hunger (Void predates the Keeper)
The Void is the **anti-pattern that existed before structure** — the formless
appetite that reality was *built against.* The Architects did not create order
from nothing; they created order *over* the Void, the way a dam is built over a
river. The Keeper, in this theory, was the first Architect to stop fearing the
Void and start *negotiating* with it. **Implication:** the Void cannot be
destroyed, only walled out; "purification" is really re-sealing.

### Theory B — The Prison's Exhaust (Void produced by the boundary)
The Void is a **byproduct of the Architect Sacrifice itself** — the metaphysical
"radiation" thrown off when the pure Nexus Stone was fractured to build the
prison. In this theory the Void is the **scar tissue of the original wound**
(Chunk 1 §2.4: "the original wound and the original protection"). It leaks from
the seams of the prison; it is strongest where the lattice is weakest. **Implication:**
healing the lattice *reduces* the Void at the source; the Void and the prison are
the same wound seen from two sides. This is the theory most flattering to hope —
and therefore the one the Void itself most likes heroes to half-believe, because
it makes them confident they can "fix" what they are actually feeding.

### Theory C — The Keeper's Reach (Void discovered/extended by the Keeper)
The Void is **raw, will-less potential** — neither old nor exhaust — and the
Keeper, locked outside reality, learned to **pattern it** the way a prisoner
learns to send messages by tapping pipes. What looks like the Void "wanting"
something is the **Keeper's intent expressed through a will-less medium.**
**Implication:** the Void can be *interrupted* by disrupting the Keeper's
patterning, even if the medium remains.

### On will — RESOLVED (soft lock)
**The Void does not have will. It has pattern.** It **mimics will through
parasitic patterning** (Chunk 1 §16). When the Void "tempts," it is not choosing
words like a demon; it is **completing the shape of your own desire**, the way
water completes the shape of a crack. This is why Void lies are always *your*
logic, finished for you. The terror is not that something alien is speaking — it
is that it sounds exactly like the most reasonable version of yourself. *(VERDAX
narration rule: never write the Void as a character with opinions. Write it as a
mirror that agrees too quickly.)*

### On purification — PARTIALLY RESOLVED (soft lock)
**The Void cannot be "cured" like a poison.** Individual instances —
**voidglass**, a corrupted relic, a void echo, a person's `void_resonance` — can
be **resisted, contained, cleansed, or healed** at real cost (ritual, sacrifice,
soul-flow repair, severing dependency, restoring an oath). But the Void *as a
condition of reality* persists as long as the lattice is imperfect. **You contain
the Void by making yourself, your relic, or your domain less unstable** — i.e. by
denying it the instability it feeds on. *Healing the cause beats fighting the
symptom every time.*

### Left fully open (for campaign reveal)
- Whether A, B, or C is *the* truth, or whether all three describe one thing at different depths.
- Whether fully restoring the pure Nexus Stone would **starve** the Void or **release** it.
- Whether the Keeper *fears* the Void (Theory A/B) or *commands* it (Theory C).

## 5. The Three Faces — In Depth

The faces are **not modes you choose between.** Every Void interaction is all
three at once; they are named separately only so a DM can see which face is
currently *loudest.*

### 5.1 ENHANCEMENT — "The brake is off."

**What it is.** The Void does not usually grant new abilities. It **removes the
natural limit** on abilities you already have: the cooldown, the mana ceiling, the
material cost, the discipline gate, the inner price that made the act *mean*
something. The output is real and immediate. The hidden truth is that the limit
was **load-bearing.**

**Mechanics.**
- Enhancement converts a **structural cost** into a **deferred metaphysical cost.** A spell that should have cost the caster's strength now costs `identity_integrity`. A relic that should have needed a year of attunement awakens in a night, drawing the difference from `soul_flow`.
- Enhancement **scales with what you bring.** A disciplined master amplified by the Void becomes terrifyingly capable; this is the trap, because the most skilled are the most tempted (they have the most to amplify).
- Enhancement **hollows meaning.** This is canon and load-bearing: Void-amplified Radiance flame "burns hotter without the inner cost, teaching that spectacle can replace devotion." The act still *works* but stops *meaning.* Repeated, this rewrites the user's relationship to their own virtue.

**Symptoms (escalating).** Effects exceeding the caster's known capacity with no
visible exhaustion · costs that "should" have appeared but didn't · a growing
preference for the amplified version of an act over the honest one · the act
feeling *empty* afterward even when it succeeded · a quiet contempt for those who
still pay the old price.

**Example.** A Radiance flamekeeper holds a collapsing temple roof with sacred
fire far past her strength. She is not tired afterward. She should be devastated;
she feels nothing. The roof held. Next week she reaches for the amplified flame
*first*, because the honest flame now feels weak and slow by comparison.

### 5.2 PARASITE — "I'll live in the part of you that's already breaking."

**What it is.** The Void does not infect the healthy. It **feeds on instability**
— broken identity, contradictory memory, interrupted soul flow, violated oaths,
love-become-control, virtue-become-obsession. It finds what a domain or person
**already loves** and makes it **extreme**, then nests in the excess and draws
sustenance from it.

**Mechanics.**
- Parasitism requires a **host instability.** No instability, no purchase. (This is why integrity is armor: a whole identity, clean memory, and intact oath give the parasite nothing to grip.)
- Parasitism **amplifies the host's existing virtue into obsession**, because obsession is self-feeding instability. It rarely introduces a *new* desire; it overgrows an existing one.
- Parasitism creates **dependency.** Each use makes the next use feel necessary, then normal, then identity-defining. This is tracked as `void_parasitism` (the entity's hollowing) feeding off rising `void_resonance` (the user's attunement). See §7.

**Symptoms.** A single value crowding out all others · diminishing satisfaction
from the thing once loved · the sense that stopping would mean *being no one* ·
others noticing the person has become "more themselves than is healthy" · physical
or magical signs only at advanced stages (voidglass sheen on the skin, an echo in
the voice).

**Example.** A Verdance elder who loves the cycle so much she begins commanding
decay — "if rot is natural, my rot is natural." The Void did not give her the love
of cycles. It overgrew it until "the cycle" meant "my will," and now she cannot
imagine herself without that control. The parasite is comfortable.

### 5.3 LIAR — "I never told you to become evil."

**What it is.** The Void **tells partial truths.** It speaks in the user's own
voice, completing the most reasonable version of their desire (§4: it mimics will
through patterning). It never says "become evil." It says "become **effective**."
Every Void lie is *true enough to act on and incomplete enough to ruin you.*

**Mechanics.**
- Every lie has a **true core** (the benefit is real) and a **missing clause** (the cost, the cost's owner, or the cost's timing).
- Lies are **domain-tuned** (§6). The Void does not offer Radiance a Tempest temptation; it offers each domain the corruption of *its own* virtue.
- A lie is most effective under **crisis pressure**, when the honest path is slow and the stakes are mortal. (Chunk 1 §5 formula: True Virtue → Crisis Pressure → Extreme Interpretation → …)

**Symptoms.** The user can perfectly justify the choice in the domain's own moral
language · the justification gets *shorter* and *more certain* with each use · the
user reframes warnings as cowardice ("the old laws are chains") · the user begins
preemptively defending a choice no one has yet challenged.

**Example.** "Mercy that fails is worse than power that wounds." True: failed
mercy *is* a tragedy. Missing clause: the power that wounds keeps wounding after
the emergency ends, and it wounds *you* first.

> **VERDAX writing rule for all three faces:** show the benefit landing *cleanly
> and first*, then let the cost arrive *later, sideways, and partly onto someone
> else.* If a player ever feels the Void was "obviously a bad deal in the moment,"
> the Void has been written wrong.

## 6. Domain-Specific Void Lies & Corruption Vectors (All Five Domains)

The five canon Void lies (Chunk 1 §3) are the **seed.** Each is expanded below
into a full temptation pattern using the template's `VoidTemptationPattern` shape:
phrase → immediate benefit → hidden cost → identity/soul-flow/reality damage →
who accepts → stage 1/2/3 symptoms. **Yes, the Void threatens Abyss too.**

### 6.1 Radiance — *"If your sacrifice is pure, why fear burning more?"*

- **Immediate benefit:** flame burns hotter, victories are larger, martyrs become legends, the temple holds, the enemy engine dies.
- **Hidden cost:** sacrifice mutates into **spectacle**; the inner devotion that made fire sacred is bypassed and atrophies; flame legitimacy detaches from cost.
- **Identity damage:** the hero confuses *willingness to die* with *holiness of action* — "if I am willing to burn, whatever I do must be holy." Selfhood collapses into a martyr-image.
- **Soul-flow damage:** false rebirth pressure; phoenix rites strained; `void_amplification` wounds in sacred-fire users.
- **Reality damage:** Sunstone / sacred-flame instability; Radiance Stone stress.
- **Most likely to accept:** Ashen Martyrs, high-output Forgekeepers, young acolytes seeking glory.
- **Stage 1:** burning "a little past safe limits" and being praised for it.
- **Stage 2:** doctrine drift toward high-output relic use; honest fire feels weak.
- **Stage 3:** martyr addiction; demanding *others'* sacrifice as proof of devotion; spectacle replaces communion.

### 6.2 Verdance — *"If decay is part of the cycle, why not command it?"*

- **Immediate benefit:** corrupted sap stops a patrol, weaponized rot defends a refuge, "adaptation" answers a blight, the grove survives.
- **Hidden cost:** corruption is **renamed adaptation**; the cycle stops being a thing one *serves* and becomes a thing one *owns.*
- **Identity damage:** the steward becomes the controller — "if rot is natural, my rot is Verdance." Communion with living systems is replaced by command over them.
- **Soul-flow damage:** root-memory distortion; ecological soul-flow stagnation; `void_amplification` in sapcraft.
- **Reality damage:** soil/living-network corruption; Verdance Stone (living network) destabilization.
- **Most likely to accept:** the Hollow Pact, desperate refuge-holders, fatalist elders.
- **Stage 1:** "emergency-only" use of corrupted growth that quietly becomes routine.
- **Stage 2:** Rootwarden trust erodes; corruption treated as a tool, not a wound.
- **Stage 3:** elder tyranny / biological control; the cycle is commanded, not communed with; rot hoarded as a weapon.

### 6.3 Tempest — *"If freedom is sacred, no one has the right to restrain you."*

- **Immediate benefit:** total liberty of action; bonds, oaths, and duties stop slowing you; you fly where others fear.
- **Hidden cost:** freedom curdles into **entitlement and anti-responsibility**; chosen bond (the heart of Tempest) is reframed as a chain to escape.
- **Identity damage:** the rider becomes "the one who cannot be restrained" — but a self defined only by *refusing limits* has no interior; it dissolves into appetite.
- **Soul-flow damage:** bond-law stress; broken-oath wounds; severed dragon-partnership soul ties.
- **Reality damage:** sky-motion instability; Tempest Stone stress; storms that don't obey their own season.
- **Most likely to accept:** Chainbreaker Radicals, corsairs, clan-arrogant riders, the jilted and the proud.
- **Stage 1:** "no one has the right to stop me" as a thrilling private creed.
- **Stage 2:** oath rejection; possessive love reframed as "free" love; responsibility called cowardice.
- **Stage 3:** bond domination — *forcing* a dragon's obedience while calling it freedom; the contradiction that hollows the self.

### 6.4 Sanctuary — *"If protection matters, you cannot allow dangerous people to choose freely."*

- **Immediate benefit:** the threat is contained, the city is safe, the dangerous choice is prevented before it harms anyone, the oath is enforced.
- **Hidden cost:** protection becomes **control**; mercy and trust — the substance of Sanctuary — are sacrificed to the *appearance* of safety. The Void destroys trust *while the user believes they are defending it.*
- **Identity damage:** the protector becomes the warden; selfhood reorganizes around suspicion ("to protect people I must control their dangerous choices").
- **Soul-flow damage:** broken-oath corrosion (oaths twisted into coercion); identity-dissolution in the surveilled.
- **Reality damage:** Beacon / Light Eternal harmony drop; Sanctuary Stone stress as civic trust collapses.
- **Most likely to accept:** Purity Inquisition hardliners, frightened civic authorities, oath-keepers under siege.
- **Stage 1:** one "reasonable" surveillance measure during a real crisis.
- **Stage 2:** purity trials, forced conformity, public shame as policy; mercy called weakness.
- **Stage 3:** authoritarian protection; legal cruelty performed as virtue; the protected become the imprisoned.

### 6.5 Abyss — *"The Keeper's order is too slow. Dissolve the world and become the truth beneath law."*

**Yes — the Void threatens Abyss.** Abyss is the Keeper's organized instrument; it
believes in **order, ledger, ownership, administered death.** The Void offers
something *more total than order:* **dissolution.** It tempts Abyssians to stop
*administering* reality and start *unmaking* it — to skip the patient bureaucracy
of soul-debt and just **become the solvent.** This is the Void betraying even the
Keeper's tool, because the Void is loyal to nothing.

- **Immediate benefit:** instant results without the ledger's slowness; power that doesn't wait for contracts, courts, or hierarchy; the rival administrator bypassed.
- **Hidden cost:** the **infrastructure of Abyssian power dissolves** — ledgers, contracts, and soul-debt require *stable* reality to enforce; the accelerationist who "becomes the solvent" destroys the very system that made Abyssian evil *effective.*
- **Identity damage:** the administrator becomes a hollow that cannot administer; "the truth beneath law" has no self, only appetite.
- **Soul-flow damage:** mass identity-dissolution; collapse of the death-passage *control* Abyss profits from (Abyss wants death **controlled**, not **dissolved**).
- **Reality damage:** the most severe of all five — Abyssian Void acceleration drops `reality_stability` fastest, because it weaponizes dissolution *on purpose.*
- **Most likely to accept:** Abyssian accelerationists, disgraced ledger-priests, those who lost a power struggle within Abyss's hierarchy and want to burn the board.
- **Stage 1:** impatience with procedure; "the contract is too slow, take it directly."
- **Stage 2:** skipping the ledger; raw dissolution used where binding was the doctrine; superiors alarmed.
- **Stage 3:** an accelerationist cell that wants to dissolve reality *now* — which the Keeper opposes, because the Keeper wants to **rule** reality, not lose it to the solvent. **The Void will eat the Keeper's house too.**

> **Locked nuance:** the Abyssian Void lie is the proof that the Void is **not**
> "just the Keeper's magic." A faithful Abyssian and a Void accelerationist want
> *different end-states* — administered ownership vs. total dissolution. The
> Keeper sits between them, using both, fearing the second.

## 7. Void Resonance & Dependency Progression

The Void's grip is tracked across three state fields and progresses in **three
stages** for any host. The fields (from Chunk 1, Chunk 3):

- **`void_resonance`** — how *attuned* a host is to the Void; how easily it can reach them and how strong its enhancements land. Rises with use, with proximity to voidglass/echoes/corrupted relics, and with unresolved instability. (Character field; also tracked per faction/domain/relic.)
- **`identity_integrity`** — selfhood stability. The Void *spends* this to fund enhancement and *erodes* it through parasitism. Low integrity = more host instability = more parasite purchase.
- **`void_parasitism`** — how much the Void is **living off** the host: dependency depth, hollowing, how much of the host's function is now Void-sustained rather than self-sustained. (Domain/faction/global field; mirrored at character level via dependency.)

**Coupling rule:** rising `void_resonance` + falling `identity_integrity` ⇒
rising `void_parasitism`. Resonance is the door; low integrity is the unlocked
latch; parasitism is the tenant that moves in.

### Stage table (by host type)

| Host | **Stage 1 — Footing** (resonance ~1–25) | **Stage 2 — Dependency** (resonance ~26–60) | **Stage 3 — Hollowing** (resonance ~61–100) |
|---|---|---|---|
| **A person** | One useful use, justified. No visible cost. `identity_integrity` −1 to −3. Slight contempt for the honest path. | Reaches for the amplified act first. Honest path feels weak. `identity_integrity` falling steadily; `void_parasitism` now nonzero. Memory of *why* they started blurs. | Cannot imagine self without it. Voidglass sheen; echo in the voice; a single value crowds out all others. Stopping feels like ceasing to exist. `identity_integrity` critical. |
| **An NPC** | Acts slightly out of character "for good reason." Witnesses note it. | Faction-visible: takes risks the doctrine forbids; defends them preemptively. Becomes a **vector** to peers. | A corruption vessel: spreads the lie as gospel; may become an **echo-NPC** (§8) if reality is thin around them. Death/exposure releases a soul-flow wound. |
| **A faction** | One cell tolerates "emergency" Void methods. Leadership unaware or permissive. | Doctrine drift: the extreme interpretation becomes a recognized internal position; recruitment rises among the desperate. Internal `void_parasitism` rising. | The faction *is* the corruption: it can no longer pursue its goal without Void methods; its original virtue is hollow. It now destabilizes its own domain. |
| **A domain** | Isolated Void incidents; `void_parasitism` low; `domain_stone_stability` healthy but a seam appears. | Multiple factions normalize amplification; soul-flow integrity drops; Stone stress measurable; rumors of "corrupted [virtue]." | Domain virtue inverted at scale; Stone near corruption threshold; the domain wins battles while becoming easier to exploit (high military, low soul-flow). |
| **A relic** | `void_resonance` low; powers slightly exceed their making; loyalty fine. | `corruption_level` rising; relic "hungers" (Chunk 3 `RelicState.hunger`); demands use; backlash conditions form. | Corrupted relic / overcharge artifact (§9): it uses the wielder as much as the wielder uses it; awakening it fully is a depth-5 event. |

**Reversal at any stage** (per §4's "resist/contain/cleanse," never "cure"): deny
the parasite its instability. **Restore the cause, not just the symptom** —
repair the broken oath, heal the soul-flow wound, sever the dependency, cleanse
the voidglass, re-anchor the identity. Reversal is *real* but *costs* (ritual,
sacrifice, time, soul-flow repair) and is **harder at each later stage.** Stage 3
reversal usually requires losing something the host values as much as the Void
gave them.

## 8. Void Echoes — The Reality-Thin Phenomena

Where the Void is used heavily or repeatedly, **`reality_stability` drops
locally**, and the thinned seam lets through **Void Echoes:** false memories,
mimic-voices, echo-NPCs, and false paths. Echoes are tracked in
`CampaignMetaphysics.active_void_echoes` and surface as `RumorState.source.type:
"void_echo"`.

**Echoes are not ghosts and not illusions in the ordinary sense.** They are
**reality completing a pattern that isn't fully there** — the same parasitic
patterning that makes the Void "speak" (§4), now bleeding into the environment.
An echo is *plausible* the way a Void lie is plausible: it is made of true-feeling
material assembled into a false whole.

### Manifestations

- **False memories.** A person, place, or faction "remembers" an event that did not happen (or a real event the wrong way). Tracked as `memory_stability` drops and `NPCMemoryState.accuracy` falling with `can_be_distorted: true`. *Player-facing:* an NPC swears the player promised something they never said. *Hidden:* it's a void echo, born of a nearby reality-thin seam.
- **Mimic-voices.** A trusted voice (a dead ally, a commander, the player's own inner voice) heard saying exactly the most persuasive thing. This is the §5.3 Liar made audible. Mimic-voices *never* say anything the listener couldn't have rationalized themselves — that's the tell, available only to the wary.
- **Echo-NPCs.** Where reality is very thin, a pattern coheres into a *person* — a stranger who knows too much, fits too well, appears at the perfect moment with the perfect offer. An echo-NPC is the Void wearing a face. They can give real aid (the benefit is real!) and pass entirely for human until a soul-flow probe, a true-name test, or a reality-stability anomaly exposes them. Tracked as an `NPCState` with `visibility_to_player: hidden` and a hidden `private_role`.
- **False paths.** A route, a door, a "way through" that *is there* and *does function* — and leads somewhere the lattice did not intend, draining `reality_stability` from whoever travels it. The shortcut that works (§1), made geographic.

### Echo mechanics

- Echoes **require** low local `reality_stability`. Heal the seam (reduce nearby Void use, cleanse voidglass, restore soul flow) and echoes fade.
- Echoes **feed** instability: believing a false memory, obeying a mimic-voice, trusting an echo-NPC, or taking a false path each drops `reality_stability` *further*, widening the seam — a self-reinforcing loop.
- Echoes are a **rumor engine.** A void_echo rumor is especially dangerous because it has no human source to discredit and a true-feeling core.
- **Reveal levels** apply (§16): an echo is `hidden` until a probe, anomaly, or contradiction promotes it to `hinted` → `rumored` → `partial` → `confirmed`.

## 9. Voidglass, Corrupted Relics & Overcharge — The Material Face

The Void is mostly a *condition*, but it leaves **physical residue.** This is the
DM's most tangible handle on it.

### 9.1 Voidglass
The crystallized residue of heavy Void use — a black, faintly mirror-bright
material that forms where reality has been spent (battlefields of Void-amplified
combat, the floor beneath an overcharged relic, the skin of a Stage-3 host). It is
**not inert:**
- Proximity raises `void_resonance` in people, relics, and locations near it.
- It is a **reagent**: ground voidglass amplifies a spell or relic the way the Void amplifies anything — really, and at hidden cost.
- It **lowers local `reality_stability`**, seeding echoes (§8).
- It can be **cleansed** (soul-flow ritual, Radiance purgation done *with discipline*, Verdance root-memory restoration) — but cleansing is itself costly and, done with Void shortcuts, ironically produces more voidglass.

### 9.2 Corrupted relics
Any relic (Chunk 3 `RelicState`) with rising `void_resonance` and `corruption_level`.
Because relics are **memory-bearing objects**, the Void's parasitism gives them
something like appetite (`hunger`):
- The relic's `powers_known` exceed its making; its `powers_hidden` are Void-funded and carry `backlash_conditions`.
- Selfish or amplified use lowers `loyalty` and raises `hunger` and `corruption_level`.
- A fully Void-awakened relic **uses the wielder** — funding its effects from the wielder's `identity_integrity` and `soul_integrity`. Awakening it is a **depth-5** event.
- Cleansing a corrupted relic is possible but may cost the relic's powers, the wielder's bond, or a soul-flow price.

### 9.3 Overcharge
The **act** that produces voidglass and corrupts relics: pushing a magic system,
a Stone-derived source, or a relic past its honest limit *via* the Void.
Overcharge is the verb form of Enhancement (§5.1). It always delivers
(over-output is real) and always defers (the brake it removed was load-bearing).
Repeated overcharge is the fastest route from Stage 1 to Stage 3, and the surest
way to drop a location's `reality_stability` into echo territory.

## 10. How the Void Interacts With Each Magic System

The Void is a **universal enhancement vector**: it can attach to *any* magic
system because it amplifies whatever is brought to it. The template's
`MagicSystemState.void_enhancement_vector` for each domain:

| Domain magic | What the Void offers it (enhancement vector) | What it hollows / hidden cost |
|---|---|---|
| **Radiance — sacred fire / phoenix rites / Sunstone craft** | Hotter flame, larger rebirth, victory without exhaustion; overcharged Sunstone relics. | Sacrifice → spectacle; phoenix rites → false rebirth pressure; flame legitimacy detaches from inner cost. |
| **Verdance — sapcraft / root-memory / living networks** | Command over decay; corrupted sap as weapon/medicine; "adaptation" instead of cleansing. | Communion → control; root-memory distortion; soil and living-network corruption; ecological stagnation. |
| **Tempest — storm-calling / dragon bond / sky-motion** | Bonds and oaths bypassed; storms forced; restraint of partners "made easy." | Chosen bond → domination; bond-law stress; severed soul ties; storms that defy season. |
| **Sanctuary — oath-magic / Light Eternal / Beacon wards** | Oaths enforced by compulsion; wards that surveil; "safety" engineered fast. | Oath → coercion; trust → control; Beacon harmony drop; the warded become the imprisoned. |
| **Abyss — soul-binding / memory-editing / death infrastructure** | Skip the ledger; raw dissolution instead of patient binding; instant results. | The *control* Abyss profits from collapses into *dissolution*; reality_stability drops fastest; even the Keeper's instrument is hollowed. |
| **Soul magic (cross-domain)** | Direct, immediate manipulation of life-thread, memory, identity, name, rebirth — *the* Void favorite, because soul magic already touches the lattice. | Routes the cost straight through soul flow and the prison; the deepest wounds (`identity_dissolution`, `name_erasure`, `death_passage_blocked`) cluster here. |

**General rule:** the Void enhances by **borrowing against the system's own
foundation.** The more foundational the magic (soul magic, Stone-craft), the
larger the enhancement *and* the deeper the hidden cost. This is why the most
powerful traditions are the most dangerous to amplify.

## 11. Cross-Domain Infiltration — Cells, Whisperers, Accelerationists

> **Template adaptation:** the Void has **no civilization, no faction index of its
> own, no homeland.** Instead of a "Faction Index," this section catalogs the
> **shapes the Void takes *inside* other domains' factions.** These are not a Void
> army. They are cells, individuals, and tendencies that the Void *infiltrates and
> grows*, made of ordinary domain members who each believe they are still serving
> their own domain.

### Infiltration archetypes (appear in every domain, domain-flavored)

- **The Accelerationist.** Believes the honest path is too slow for the crisis. Uses Void methods "until the emergency passes" — and the emergency never passes. (Radiance: burn faster. Verdance: command the rot now. Tempest: refuse all restraint. Sanctuary: control first, trust later. Abyss: dissolve, don't administer.)
- **The Whisperer.** A vector NPC at Stage 2 who spreads the lie as wisdom — often charismatic, often *right about the problem* and wrong about the cure. Rarely knows they are a Void vector. The most dangerous because most sincere.
- **The Cell.** A small group inside a legitimate faction that has normalized Void methods among themselves (e.g. a Forgekeeper overcharge clique, a Hollow Pact sap-corruption ring, a Chainbreaker bond-domination crew, a Purity Inquisition surveillance bureau, an Abyssian accelerationist conclave). Cells are deniable, recruit the desperate, and hollow their host faction from within.
- **The Vessel.** A Stage-3 corruption vessel (the template's `villain/corruption vessel` NPC role): a person the Void has so thoroughly hollowed that they actively, knowingly spread it — and may have become, or be becoming, an echo-NPC.
- **The Unwitting Patron.** A leader or institution that *protects* a cell because the cell delivers results. Provides cover without (yet) understanding the cost. The bridge by which a Stage-1 incident becomes Stage-3 domain rot.

### Where they nest (by domain)

- **Radiance:** Ashen Martyrs fringe; high-output Forgekeeper cliques; glory-seeking acolyte circles.
- **Verdance:** the Hollow Pact (the clearest organized Void-adjacent tendency); desperate refuge-holders; fatalist elder courts.
- **Tempest:** Chainbreaker Radicals; corsair crews; bond-domination rings hiding inside legitimate Sanctums.
- **Sanctuary:** Purity Inquisition hardliner bureaus; emergency-powers councils; surveillance wardens who began as protectors.
- **Abyss:** accelerationist conclaves; disgraced ledger-priests; faction-war losers who want to dissolve the board. **(These the Keeper actively fears.)**

> **Locked:** the Void is **not** a unified conspiracy. Cells in different domains
> usually do not coordinate; each thinks it is solving its *own* domain's problem.
> The Void's "organization" is *emergent* — the same patterning (§4) producing
> similar shapes independently. (The Keeper may *attempt* to coordinate them; the
> Void itself does not.)

## 12. Relationship Logic — How Temptation Enters and Is Resisted (≥10 IF/THEN/BECAUSE)

Using the canon `IF / THEN / BECAUSE` form (Chunk 1 §8, Chunk 4). Each rule names
the **face** in play and a **resistance lever.**

1. **IF** a hero faces a mortal crisis the honest path cannot solve in time, **THEN** the Void offers a real, working shortcut, **BECAUSE** crisis pressure is the Liar's best soil and the honest path's slowness is the lie's whole leverage. *(Resist: pre-commit to the cost of the honest path before the crisis — decide what you will lose rather than amplify.)*

2. **IF** a person's `identity_integrity` is high and their oaths intact, **THEN** the parasite finds no purchase even when resonance is offered, **BECAUSE** parasitism feeds only on instability; a whole self gives it nothing to grip.

3. **IF** an act is amplified once and praised, **THEN** the next honest act will feel weak by comparison, **BECAUSE** Enhancement hollows meaning and trains the user to prefer spectacle. *(Resist: deliberately perform the honest version next, at cost, to re-anchor.)*

4. **IF** a domain is under existential war pressure, **THEN** its accelerationist cell gains recruits and cover, **BECAUSE** results buy forgiveness for methods, and patrons protect what delivers.

5. **IF** an NPC begins preemptively defending a choice no one challenged, **THEN** they are at Stage 2 dependency, **BECAUSE** the Liar's certainty grows shorter and louder as the parasite deepens. *(Resist/diagnose: a clean conscience does not rehearse its defense.)*

6. **IF** a relic's output exceeds its making with no felt cost, **THEN** it is drawing on the wielder's integrity, **BECAUSE** Enhancement defers structural cost into metaphysical cost paid later, usually by the wielder. *(Resist: submit the relic for cleansing before replication spreads it.)*

7. **IF** local `reality_stability` drops below the echo threshold, **THEN** false memories, mimic-voices, echo-NPCs, and false paths appear, **BECAUSE** the thinned seam lets the Void complete patterns that aren't fully there.

8. **IF** a mimic-voice says exactly the most persuasive thing in a trusted voice, **THEN** suspect a void echo, **BECAUSE** echoes never say anything the listener couldn't have rationalized themselves — too-perfect agreement is the tell.

9. **IF** a player tries to *fight* the Void as a monster, **THEN** they will fail and feed it, **BECAUSE** the Void has no will to defeat (§4); it is contained by removing the instability it feeds on, not by force.

10. **IF** a host's instability is healed (oath restored, soul-flow repaired, dependency severed, identity re-anchored), **THEN** the parasite weakens regardless of stage, **BECAUSE** denying it instability is the only thing that starves it — though Stage 3 reversal costs something the host values as much as the Void gave.

11. **IF** the benefit lands cleanly and the cost arrives later and partly onto someone else, **THEN** the Void was written correctly, **BECAUSE** a temptation that looks like an obviously bad deal in the moment is not the canon Void.

12. **IF** an Abyssian chooses dissolution over administration, **THEN** even the Keeper's instrument is hollowed and the Keeper is *opposed*, **BECAUSE** the Void is loyal to nothing and would eat the Keeper's house as readily as anyone's.

13. **IF** a faction can no longer pursue its goal without Void methods, **THEN** it has reached Stage 3 and now destabilizes its own domain, **BECAUSE** the parasite has replaced the host's self-sustaining function with Void-sustained dependency.

## 13. Cause/Effect Examples (≥5, reaching 3rd–5th order)

Each shows the Void giving **real benefit first**, then extracting a **hidden,
never-clean cost** — one per domain. These extend the canon worked chains
(Chunk 4 §A–E) through the §5 three-faces lens.

### 13.1 Radiance — Overcharged victory *(canon-aligned with Chunk 4 D)*
**Root:** player destroys an Abyss soul-harvest engine with Void-amplified Radiance flame. *Benefit is real: captives saved, engine dead.*
- **1st:** captives saved; Radiance reputation +15; Abyss control −10; `void_resonance` +4; no felt exhaustion (Enhancement).
- **2nd:** Ashen Martyrs praise "burning past safe limits"; Dawnfire Mystics alarmed; Forgekeepers want the data (vector forming).
- **3rd:** acolytes imitate dangerous rites; rumor "the Phoenix's old limits are obsolete"; honest flame now feels weak to the caster.
- **4th:** a high-output relic doctrine bloc forms; Sanctuary hesitates to accept Radiance flame support; Verdance fears uninvited burning.
- **5th:** Keeper benefits — sacrifice mutates toward spectacle and Void dependency; sacred-fire instability + interdomain mistrust raise `keeper_prison_pressure`. *Hidden cost, never clean.*
- **Intervention:** publicly name the danger; submit the relic for purification; credit the captives not the spectacle; expose voidglass residue before replication.

### 13.2 Verdance — Corrupted sap *(canon-aligned with Chunk 4 B)*
**Root:** player uses corrupted-sap traps to stop an Abyss patrol at a refuge. *Benefit is real: the refuge survives.*
- **1st:** patrol destroyed; Hollow Pact respect +12; Rootwarden suspicion +10; soil corruption +4; "emergency-only" framing (Parasite footing).
- **2nd:** a Pact whisperer praises; a Rootwarden demands explanation; refugees argue survival vs. corruption.
- **3rd:** Hollow Pact recruitment rises; Sap Commons lose trust; rumor "Rootwardens secretly rely on corruption."
- **4th:** Rootwarden authority weakens; Pact cells gain shelter; Abyss studies the trap residue.
- **5th:** Verdance's resistance damages its own soul-flow; Void whispers "If rot saved them, why call it corruption?"; root-memory distortion raises Keeper pressure.
- **Intervention:** cleanse residue; name the tactic emergency-only and *enforce* it; seek a communion judgment; build a clean Sap Commons alternative.

### 13.3 Tempest — Bond control *(canon-aligned with Chunk 4 C)*
**Root:** player binds an injured dragon to stop it flying into a lethal Void-storm. *Benefit is real: the dragon lives.*
- **1st:** dragon survives; rider gratitude +15; dragon trust −20; bond-law stress +8 (the Liar: "Love that cannot restrain lets death win").
- **2nd:** the Dragon-Bond Sanctum hears of the restraint; Chainbreaker Radicals weaponize it; a bonded rider questions the player's grasp of freedom.
- **3rd:** bonded riders split; commoners cry hypocrisy; corsairs sell anti-bond restraint tools.
- **4th:** a sky-alliance mission is delayed; a dragon refuses to fly under such commanders; Abyss fortifies anti-air.
- **5th:** freedom fractures into control-vs-anti-duty extremism; severed-bond soul ties raise Keeper pressure.
- **Intervention:** submit to a Dragon-Bond trial; give the dragon public voice; find consent-restoring healing; help the rider release control.

### 13.4 Sanctuary — Protective control
**Root:** during a real threat, the player authorizes a Void-funded Beacon ward that *surveils and pre-empts* dangerous citizens. *Benefit is real: an attack is prevented, no one dies.*
- **1st:** plot foiled; Sanctuary safety +10; civic trust −6 (quietly); ward draws on Light Eternal harmony (Enhancement borrowing against the foundation).
- **2nd:** Purity Inquisition hardliners praise the "reasonable measure"; the Refugee Covenant grows afraid; a frightened councilor becomes an Unwitting Patron.
- **3rd:** surveillance normalizes; rumor "Sanctuary now punishes choices before they're made"; mercy gets called weakness in council.
- **4th:** purity trials expand; other domains distrust Sanctuary's oaths; the warded begin to resemble the imprisoned.
- **5th:** trust — Sanctuary's substance — is destroyed *while defending it*; Beacon harmony drops; broken-oath corrosion (oaths twisted into coercion) raises `keeper_prison_pressure`.
- **Intervention:** sunset the ward publicly; restore an honest oath; expose the harmony cost; rebuild trust through visible mercy at real risk.

### 13.5 Abyss — The accelerationist solvent
**Root:** an Abyssian contact, losing a ledger power-struggle, uses raw Void dissolution instead of patient soul-binding to seize a rival's holdings — and offers the player a cut. *Benefit is real: the rival is gone, the holdings transfer instantly.*
- **1st:** rival administrator dissolved; the contact's local power +12; `reality_stability` −8 (fastest-dropping vector, §6.5); voidglass forms on the site.
- **2nd:** senior ledger-priests are *alarmed*, not pleased — dissolution destroys the property they administer; an accelerationist conclave forms around the contact.
- **3rd:** the conclave skips contracts entirely; Abyssian order fractures into administrators vs. accelerationists; echoes seed at the dissolved site.
- **4th:** the death-passage *control* Abyss profits from begins to fail locally; rumor "Abyss is losing the dead it owns"; the Keeper's instrument turns on itself.
- **5th:** the Keeper is *opposed*, not advantaged, by total dissolution — yet `keeper_prison_pressure` still rises, because the lattice was spent regardless of who benefits. **The Void eats the Keeper's house too.**
- **Intervention:** cleanse the voidglass site; expose the conclave to the administrators (turn Abyss against itself); recover the dissolved souls' names; refuse the cut and let the ledger-priests act.

## 14. VERDAX Tracking Fields & VoidParasitismCheck

### 14.1 The fields that track the Void

| Field | Lives on | Meaning | Rises when |
|---|---|---|---|
| `void_resonance` | character, faction, domain, relic | attunement to the Void | Void use, voidglass/echo/corrupted-relic proximity, unresolved instability |
| `identity_integrity` | character (also conceptually NPC/faction/domain) | selfhood stability | (it *falls*) under Enhancement spend + Parasite erosion |
| `void_parasitism` | domain, faction, global (`global_void_parasitism`), char-dependency | how much the Void lives off the host | resonance up + integrity down + dependency |
| `reality_stability` | location, `global_reality_stability` | how solid local/global reality is | (it *falls*) with heavy Void use; below threshold → echoes |
| `keeper_prison_pressure` | campaign | how close the Keeper is to escape | indirectly, via Void→soul-flow→mistrust→Stone chains |
| `future_whisper` | on each `VoidTemptationEvent` | the deferred consequence/hook the Void plants | recorded whenever Void is offered, accepted, or resisted |

**Indirection rule (Chunk 3):** Keeper pressure rises through chains, not jumps:
`Void use → soul-flow damage → rumor → faction paranoia → unity drop → Stone
harmony drop → keeper_prison_pressure ↑`.

### 14.2 `VoidParasitismCheck` logic (from Chunk 4)

```ts
VoidParasitismCheck {
  triggered: boolean
  offered_lie?, immediate_benefit?, hidden_cost?: string
  void_resonance_delta?, identity_integrity_delta?, soul_flow_delta?,
    reality_stability_delta?, dependency_risk?: number

}
```

**When to trigger (`triggered: true`):** any action that (a) uses Void
enhancement/overcharge, (b) uses a corrupted relic or voidglass reagent, (c)
amplifies a magic/Stone source past its honest limit, (d) acts on a void echo, or
(e) takes a shortcut funded by soul flow / memory / identity / oath. If none of
these, `triggered: false` and the rest is omitted.

**Delta scale (Chunk 3):** ±1 subtle · ±2–3 minor · ±4–6 meaningful · ±7–10 major

- Repeated/Stage-2 use: `void_resonance` +5–8, `identity_integrity` −4–6, `reality_stability` −4–6, `dependency_risk` 30–60.
- Stage-3 / corrupted-relic awakening / Abyssian dissolution: `void_resonance` +9+, `identity_integrity` −7+, `reality_stability` −7–20, `dependency_risk` 70–100; **depth-5** event.
- A **resisted** temptation still records the event with `accepted:false, resisted:true`, near-zero deltas, and a `future_whisper` (the Void remembers the door it found).

## 16. Player-Facing vs. Hidden Handling

The Void must **feel tempting and mysterious** in play and **fully accounted** in
the DM War Room. Reveal levels (Chunk 3): `hidden → hinted → rumored → partial →
confirmed → mythic_truth`.

### What the player sees (Basic Panel)
- The **benefit, cleanly and first.** "The wall holds. The child breathes. The engine is ash."
- A **note, not a spreadsheet:** a soul-flow flicker, a new rumor seed, an advanced clock, an odd line of dialogue — *hinted* at most.
- An **expanded "why" drawer** on request, in-world: "The flame answered faster than it should have. You felt nothing afterward. You are not sure that is good."
- The temptation phrased as **the player's own most reasonable voice** (§5.3), never as a cackling villain.

### What the DM War Room holds (hidden)
- The full `VoidTemptationEvent` with all deltas and the `future_whisper`.
- `void_resonance` / `identity_integrity` / `void_parasitism` / `reality_stability` current values and trajectory; current resonance **stage** (§7).
- Echo seeds: which locations are near the echo threshold; which NPCs risk becoming echo-NPCs (§8).
- Keeper-pressure chain in progress (the indirect route the benefit is taking toward `keeper_prison_pressure`).
- Cell/whisperer/patron tracking (§11): who is at which stage, who protects whom.

### Reveal escalation rules
- A first use is `hidden`. Repetition, voidglass formation, an echo, an NPC's changed behavior, or a probe promotes it: `hinted` → `rumored` (a void_echo or witness rumor) → `partial` (a healer/seer names it) → `confirmed` (cleansing or inquiry proves it) → `mythic_truth` (the campaign's Source answer at `keeper_revelation`).
- **Never reveal hidden cost the player hasn't earned discovering.** The Void's mystery *is* its danger; a fully-disclosed cost is no longer a temptation.

## 17. Open Questions

- **Source (left open by design, §4):** Is the Void the Older Hunger (A), the Prison's Exhaust (B), or the Keeper's Reach (C) — or one thing seen at three depths? A campaign may reveal an answer at `keeper_revelation`.
- **Restoration paradox:** Would restoring the pure Nexus Stone **starve** the Void (Theory B) or **release** it (Theory A)? This is the highest-stakes open question and a candidate `final_convergence` dilemma.
- **Keeper's relation:** Does the Keeper **fear** the Void (A/B) or **command** it (C)? The Abyssian-dissolution lie (§6.5) suggests at minimum that the Keeper does not *fully* command it.
- **Echo-NPC nature:** Can an echo-NPC ever become *genuinely* a person (gain real soul flow), or are they always patterns? Can one be redeemed, or only dispelled?
- **Voidglass economy:** Will any faction try to *industrialize* voidglass as a reagent (an Abyssian or Forgekeeper temptation), and what does a Void-reagent black market do to a region's `reality_stability`?
- **Resistance traditions:** Does any domain hold a genuine, non-corrupting method of Void *containment* (e.g. a Verdance root-memory cleansing, a disciplined Radiance purgation), or is all "anti-Void" power itself a Void vector if pushed?
- **Can a host at Stage 3 ever fully recover**, or does Stage-3 reversal always leave a permanent scar (a floor on `identity_integrity`)?

## 18. Locked Decisions

1. **The Void is a cross-domain corruption LAYER, not a domain.** No Stone, no government, no economy, no army, no homeland, no people.
2. **The Void is enhancement, parasite, and liar** — simultaneously; also amplifier, identity solvent, reality destabilizer, false shortcut.
3. **The Void gives real benefits but never clean power.** The benefit is genuine and lands first; the cost is hidden, deferred, and never clean.
4. **The Void is NOT "evil magic" and NOT "purple corruption fog."** It is the shortcut that works.
5. **The Void is NOT the origin of Abyssian evil.** Abyss's root is the Keeper; the Void only made Abyss more powerful, unstable, and reality-damaging.
6. **The Void threatens all five domains, including Abyss.** The Abyssian lie (dissolution over order) proves the Void is loyal to nothing — it would eat the Keeper's house too.
7. **The Void has no will; it has pattern.** It mimics will through parasitic patterning, completing the user's own desire. Never written as a character with opinions — written as a mirror that agrees too quickly.
8. **Void temptation must always sound plausible**, in the user's own most reasonable voice and in the target domain's own moral language. A temptation that looks like an obviously bad deal in the moment is mis-written.
9. **The Void cannot be "cured," only resisted, contained, cleansed, or healed at real cost** — by denying it the instability it feeds on. Heal the cause, not just the symptom; reversal is harder at each later stage.
10. **A heroic action can create metaphysical damage through Void methods.** Intent does not refund the lattice that was spent.
11. **Void use is tracked via** `VoidTemptationEvent`, `void_resonance`, `identity_integrity`, `void_parasitism`, `reality_stability`, and a planted `future_whisper`; **`VoidParasitismCheck` runs on any qualifying action.**
12. **Keeper pressure rises indirectly** from Void use, through soul-flow/rumor/faction/Stone chains — never as a random jump.
13. **Heavy/repeated Void use drops local `reality_stability` and seeds Void Echoes** (false memories, mimic-voices, echo-NPCs, false paths).
14. **The hidden cost is stored for the DM War Room; the player feels temptation and mystery.** Never disclose a cost the player hasn't earned discovering.
15. **The Void's "organization" is emergent, not a unified conspiracy** — independent cells, whisperers, accelerationists, and unwitting patrons inside other domains, each believing they still serve their own domain. The Keeper may attempt to coordinate them; the Void does not.
