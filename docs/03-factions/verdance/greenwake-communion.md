# The Greenwake Communion — *Those Who Truly Listen*

> "The elders say they interpret the roots. We say: stop interpreting, and HEAR.
> The dead are not a doctrine to be argued. They are a grief in the dark, calling
> a name, waiting for someone to answer. I have answered. Now I cannot stop
> hearing them — and that, child, is both the gift and the wound."
> — Auralis Greenwake, in the Listening Hollow beneath Thornveil

---

## 1. Faction Identity Snapshot

The Greenwake Communion is not a church and not a guild. It is an **order of
listeners** — communers, grief-tenders, and spiritually-gifted refugees scattered
across the classes of Verdance, bound by one heretical-sounding conviction: that
**the roots and the dead speak directly to those who truly listen, and not only
through the elders' interpretation.** Where the Heartmoot drinks the shared sap and
declares what the cycle *means*, the Communion drinks and falls silent and *hears* —
and increasingly, what it hears does not match what the elders say.

The Communion exists because Verdance's holiest art, **Root-Communion**, was quietly
captured by its interpreters (the Heartmoot's slide toward elder tyranny, Verdance
Bible §7.2). The Communion is the revolt of the direct-hearers against the
interpreters — a revival movement that insists communion is a **relationship, not a
doctrine.** Its members are loved by the people and feared by the council, because
their hearing is a legitimacy the elders cannot control: if Auralis Greenwake hears
the dead truly and contradicts the Heartmoot, the council's interpretive monopoly
cracks.

And at Thornveil, where a corrupted rootwell has begun **speaking names**, the
Communion's gift becomes indispensable and perilous at once. They are the **only
ones who can actually communicate with the trapped voices** — the only faction that
can tell the player *what the well is saying* and *who is caught in it.* But to hear
the trapped is to be begged by them. And the Void waits inside that begging, offering
Auralis the one thing they want most: to free the dead — the fast way, by *using*
them.

**One-paragraph pitch.** The Greenwake Communion is the player's lantern in the
mystery and the player's slowest-burning danger. They are gentle, beloved, and
right about almost everything: the elders *have* been hiding the sealed wells, the
trapped *are* real, communion *is* holier than interpretation. They will hand the
player the answer to the central question — *whose names is the well speaking, and
why?* — that no one else can. But the same gift that makes them indispensable makes
Auralis Greenwake the person the Void most wants. Every voice they hear is a soul
pleading to be freed, and the Void's whole game in Verdance is to make *mercy the
bait.* The Communion is what the Hollow Pact would have been if it had stayed kind
one season longer — and the Hollow Pact is what the Communion becomes if it stops
listening and starts *commanding.* The two are the same hearing pointed at opposite
verbs: **release** versus **use.**

## 2. Doctrine (Locked Faction Canon)

These statements bind. They obey Verdance Bible §19 (Religion) and §20 (Faction
Index) and may be expanded but not contradicted.

1. **Direct hearing over interpretation.** The roots and the dead speak directly to
   those who truly listen. The elders' interpretation is a service, not a monopoly,
   and where it contradicts what the dead actually say, the dead are right.
2. **Communion is the highest art.** Above governance, above containment, above the
   market — to listen together, truly, is the holiest Verdant work (Verdance Bible
   §6.3, Communion).
3. **The trapped must be HEARD, then RELEASED.** A blocked passage is the deepest
   horror (§19, relationship_to_death). The Communion's vocation is to hear the held
   dead, learn their true names, and complete their passage — the slow, true way.
4. **The cycle is a relationship, not a doctrine.** One does not *believe in* the
   cycle the way one believes a creed; one *lives within it* and tends it like a bond.
   Doctrine ossifies; relationship listens and adapts.
5. **Release is never command.** This is the Communion's own internal taboo and its
   thin line against the Void: the dead are freed by being *heard and let go*, never
   by being *used.* To use the trapped — even to free them — is the cardinal
   violation wearing a kind face.
6. **No one listens alone (in principle).** Communion is communal; a single hearer
   who acts unilaterally on what they alone heard has begun to drift toward the
   elders' very sin in reverse — and toward the Void.
7. **Auralis Greenwake leads but does not rule.** The Communion has no rootmoot, no
   council, no enforced hierarchy — authority flows from the quality of one's
   listening (§6.3, political_expression). Auralis leads because they hear deepest,
   not because they command.
8. **The sealed wells are an open wound, not a heritage.** The Communion holds, as
   doctrine, that the Heartmoot's sealed-and-denied rootwells are abandonment of the
   dead (the cardinal sin, §7.2 late_stage), not mercy — because the Communion can
   still *hear* those dead crying from the sealed wells.

> **Doctrinal danger summary.** Every clause above is true and good — and clause 5
> is the only thing standing between the Communion and the Hollow Pact. The Keeper's
> entire Verdance play against this faction is to erode clause 5 with clauses 3 and 4:
> *you must free them (3), the cycle is a relationship so adapt (4) — so free them
> by using them.* The collapse of "release is never command" into "release by
> command" IS the merciful heresy.

## 3. Class Base

```ts
class_base: {
  "Communers & Listeners (across classes)": 45,   // the Listening Class, but drawn from every walk of Verdant life
  "Spiritually-gifted refugees": 25,              // the Sheltered who arrived with the gift — grief made them hear
  "Tenders (healers, grove-keepers, Passage-Tenders)": 20, // Ilyra Thornsong's kind; the ones whose rites are failing
  "Disillusioned ex-elders / lapsed interpreters": 10      // those who left the Heartmoot to hear directly
}
```

**Reading the class base.** The Communion is **trans-class** — its gift does not
respect the Heartmoot's hierarchy. A refugee child can hear deeper than a senior
elder, which is precisely why the elders fear it. Its center of gravity is the
**grief-touched**: refugees whose dead are in the wells, Passage-Tenders whose rites
have stopped working, healers who can no longer release the town's dead. This is also
its vulnerability — a faction built of the freshly bereaved is a faction one despair
away from the Hollow Pact, where the bereaved-and-furious gather (Verdance Bible
§7.6, §9). The Communion and the Hollow Pact recruit from the **same grief.** The
difference is the verb.

Scored 0–100 per the Chunk 3 numeric standard (0–10 absent · 11–20 critical · 21–40
weak · 41–60 contested · 61–80 strong · 81–95 dominant · 96–100 mythic).

```ts
power_sources: {
  military: 8,        // near-absent. The Communion has no soldiers, no thornwall, no force. It listens; it does not fight. (Contrast Rootwardens.)
  economy: 22,        // weak. Sustained by devotion-offerings and the labor of its tender-members; owns no markets, no seed-stock, no rootwell rationing. Materially poor, which keeps it honest.
  magic: 88,          // DOMINANT and singular. Unmatched depth of Root-Communion; the ONLY practitioners who can hear and speak with the trapped voices. This is the faction's entire weight.
  religion: 82,       // dominant spiritual legitimacy — popular devotion treats Auralis's hearings as near-prophecy (§24 trusted_sources). Rivals the elders' OWN religious claim, which is the conflict.
  intelligence: 64,   // strong but strange. Their "intelligence" is what the dead and the roots tell them — true, but ambiguous (oracle vs memory vs Void lie, §24). They know things no spy could learn, and some of it is a trap.
  legitimacy: 71,     // strong popular/spiritual legitimacy; CONTESTED institutional legitimacy (the Heartmoot brands them heretics, §7.2 visible_symptoms). Legitimacy that flows from the people, not the council.
  resources: 30,      // weak-to-contested. No seed-vaults, no clean-sap stockpile, no Heartroot cuttings (those are locked in the capital, controlled by the Heartmoot). Their one irreplaceable "resource" is access to the trapped voices.
  fear: 18            // low and intentional. The Communion is loved, not feared — by design. Its only "fear" is the unease its direct hearing provokes in the elders, and the dread of what it might become.
}
```

**The shape of this power.** The Communion is the most **lopsided** faction in
Verdance: near-zero military, economy, resources, and fear; dominant magic and
religion. It cannot hold a wall, ration sap, or field a soldier — but it holds the
**one key every other faction needs:** the meaning of the well. The Rootwardens can
contain the well but not understand it; the Sap Commons can cure a wound but not
know which dead are in it; the Hollow Pact can *use* the voices but lies to itself
about freeing them; the Seed Guild can sell around it; the Heartmoot can wait. Only
the Communion can answer **"who is trapped, and what do they need to be released?"** —
which is the question the whole MVP turns on. Its weakness everywhere else is what
makes it *non-threatening enough to trust* and *dependent enough to be used* — by the
player, by every faction, and by the Void.

## 5. Methods

```ts
methods: {
  diplomacy: 55,            // moderate. Persuades through hearing and shared grief, not negotiation; mediates between cost-bearers and elders; poor at hard bargaining.
  open_war: 5,             // none. The Communion does not make war.
  sabotage: 12,            // negligible, and against doctrine; at most, withholding an interpretation or quietly contradicting an elder's reading.
  propaganda: 60,          // strong but soft — its "hearings" spread as near-prophecy (§24). It does not lie, but a true hearing repeated IS a social weapon. Its danger is sincerity, not spin.
  assassination: 2,        // none.
  trade_pressure: 8,       // none worth noting; it has nothing to embargo.
  magical_escalation: 70   // high POTENTIAL, dangerous. Its deepest communion can do what nothing else can (true Passage-completion) — but pushed past the line, the same magic becomes the Void's false Passage Tending (release-by-use). Its escalation is spiritual, not martial.
}
```

**The method that matters.** The Communion's real instrument is **magical_escalation
in the form of communion itself** — and it cuts both ways. Used rightly, deep
communion is the *cure*: hear the trapped, learn the true name, Name them Back,
release them (§5 healing_methods, §25 if_then_because). Used past the line, the exact
same act becomes the heresy: *command* the cycle to release them, *use* them to free
them. There is no clean wall between the Communion's holiest method and its worst sin
— only Auralis's restraint. That is the faction's whole drama.

is a quieter clock, `clock_greenwake_vs_heartmoot`, tracking whether the Communion's

are coupled: empowering the Communion without anchoring Auralis is how a victory
becomes a catastrophe.

goodness**, never as a fall into wickedness.

### Chain A — The player helps Auralis "free" the trapped by USING them (the merciful heresy)

  Passage-completion to prove the slow way works (Chain B); expose the "release" as a
  binding (have the well speak the "freed" name again, proving it was never freed);

## 14. MVP Role in *The Sap Beneath the Ash*

The Greenwake Communion is **indispensable to the MVP and dangerous to it in the same
breath.** Its role is structural, not optional.

**Indispensable to the mystery.** The central question of *The Sap Beneath the Ash* is
*why is the Thornveil rootwell speaking names — whose names, and what does it want?*
Every other faction has a POSITION on the well (contain, heal, weaponize, stabilize,
wait) but **only the Greenwake Communion can READ it.** The Rootwardens can wall the
well but not understand it; the Sap Commons can attempt a cure but not know which dead
are in it or what they need; the Hollow Pact can *use* the voices but lies to itself
about what using does; the Seed Guild can sell around it; the Heartmoot can interpret
it conveniently. The Communion — Auralis — is the **only source of ground truth**:
these are real, suffering, named souls; here is who they were; here is what they need
to be released. Without the Communion, the player is investigating a haunting blind.
With it, the mystery becomes solvable. VERDAX should route the player's investigation
*through* the Communion at every major beat — and make the player feel the dependency.

**The dark mirror the Hollow Pact distorts.** The Communion and the Hollow Pact hear
the **same voices** and want the **same thing** — the trapped freed. The entire moral
architecture of the MVP lives in the gap between their verbs: the Communion's *release*
versus the Pact's *use* (§20, §21 Nymara, §3 shared grief). Nymara Hollowbloom is what
Auralis Greenwake becomes at corruption-clock tick 8 — the same hearing, the same love
for the dead, the same certainty that they are freeing them, arrived at by a crueler
road. Auralis is the kinder road to the same place. This makes the Communion the
**theological keystone** of the MVP: the Hollow Pact's heresy is not alien evil but a
*distortion of the Communion's holiest art*, and the player's deepest task is to keep
the Communion on the right side of one word.

**The slow-burn danger.** The Communion is the one faction whose corruption the player
will not see coming, because it is the one faction the player most needs and most
trusts. Its betrayal looks like its triumph (§10): a quiet well, a weeping grateful
crowd, a name that stops — and a soul bound tighter. VERDAX should let the player lean
on the Communion, love Auralis, depend on their hearings — and then make the slide
(§6) creep in under the cover of that dependence. The player must learn to **use the
Communion as a guide, anchor Auralis as a person, and watch — kindly, vigilantly — for
the slow slide from communion to control.**

**The good ending runs through this faction.** The strongest resolution of *The Sap
Beneath the Ash* (§23 peaceful_resolution) is a **true Passage-completion**: hear the
trapped (Communion), recover their names (Naming-Back), release them the slow true way,
while the Rootwardens contain and the Sap Commons restore and the Heartmoot's paralysis
is broken and the Abyss incision is addressed at the source. **Every clause of that
braided cure that touches the dead runs through the Greenwake Communion.** They are the
faction that makes the *good* ending possible — which is exactly why the Void wants them
so badly, and exactly why the player must guard them most.

> **MVP summary for VERDAX.** The Greenwake Communion is the lantern, the keystone, and
> the fuse. Indispensable to solving the rootwell mystery; the dark mirror the Hollow
> Pact distorts; and the slow, beloved, almost-irresistible road by which mercy becomes
> a soul-trap. Use them. Anchor Auralis. Watch for the slide.
