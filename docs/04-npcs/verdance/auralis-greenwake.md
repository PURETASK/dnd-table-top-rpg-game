# Auralis Greenwake — *The Merciful Prophet of the Wound*

> "You think the well is screaming. It isn't. It's begging. There is a difference,
> and I am the only one in this Refuge who can tell you which it is. That is not a
> gift. It is the loneliest thing I have ever carried."
> — Auralis Greenwake, to the player, at the lip of the speaking well

## Name
Auralis Greenwake. Called *the Hearer*, *the Greenwake*, *First Listener of the
Communion*. The trapped dead, increasingly, call them by name back — which is how
the slide begins.

## Domain
Verdance — Thornveil Refuge. Leader of the Greenwake Communion, the order of
direct root-hearers.

## Public Role
Spiritual leader of the Greenwake Communion: the order that holds Verdance can hear
the roots and the dead *directly*, without elder mediation. Auralis presides over
grief-communions, interprets the well's voices for the grieving, and is regarded —
by the desperate and the devout — as something close to a living oracle.

## Private Role
The only person who truly hears **what the trapped dead are saying** — their names,
their pleas, their slow decay into one another. This makes Auralis the
indispensable interpreter of the entire MVP mystery *and* the single most dangerous
person in the region, because the same gift that lets them hear the trapped is the
gift the Void will use to make them "free" the trapped by using them.

## Faction Ties
- **Greenwake Communion** — founder-leader; their authority *is* the order.
- In doctrinal conflict with the **Heartmoot** (the elders' interpretive monopoly
  vs. the Communion's direct hearing — Auralis is, to the elders, a heretic who
  contradicts their convenient interpretations).
- A mirror-rivalry with the **Hollow Pact** (Nymara): same hearing, opposite choice.
  Nymara is what Auralis becomes if compassion crosses into command. This terrifies
  Auralis, and it should.

## Belief Core
*The dead are not gone; they are held, and to hear them is a duty, not a wonder.
Mercy is the highest reading of every voice.* Auralis believes the trapped are
people, not symptoms — that they deserve to be heard, named, and released, and that
no one has the right to seal them away as the elders do. This belief is wholly good.
It is also the exact handhold the Void needs: a person who *must* help the trapped,
offered a way to "help" that damns them both.

## Desire
To **release the trapped dead** — truly, completely, into clean onward passage — and
to never again have to stand at the well listening to someone beg while the
Rootmoot debates next season. Beneath that: to not be alone in the hearing.

## Fear
That they will not be able to bear the voices much longer — and that when they break,
they will break toward *using* the dead instead of freeing them, because using them
would at least make the begging stop. Auralis fears becoming Nymara. They are right
to.

## Secret
The well has begun calling Auralis by name *back* — answering them, specifically,
the way the Void's stage-2 corruption answers a chosen vessel. They have not told
anyone, because telling Sylune or Thalen would mean being watched, or stopped, and
because some small starved part of them is *grateful* to be heard in return.
(Visibility: hidden → hinted when the player catches them answering a name that
isn't theirs; partial if romanced.)

## Public Mask
Luminous, gentle, unhurried compassion — the calm of someone who has made peace
with sorrow. They speak softly and certainly, and the grieving leave their presence
comforted. The mask is mostly real, which is what makes the cracks in it
frightening.

## Private Truth
They are exhausted to the root. The voices do not stop when the communion ends. They
have begun to drink from the well's edge "to hear more clearly," telling themselves
it is for the trapped. They are lonely in a way no living person has touched, and
the dead are the only ones who answer. They are, quietly, already on the first step
of the spiral, and they half-know it.

## Relationship Function
The player's **window into the mystery** and their slow-burning **moral tripwire**.
Auralis tells the player *what* the well is and *who* is trapped — without them, the
true cure is nearly unreachable. But every scene with Auralis is also a referendum
on whether the player will help them resist the merciful shortcut or push them
toward it. As a romance, they raise the stakes of every corruption choice to the
unbearable: loving the person most likely to fall.

## Can Romance? Betray? Die? Kill? Defect?
- **Romance:** **Yes** — the slow-burn, communal, patient Verdant kind; a romance
  that is also a vigil over someone's soul. Loving Auralis means watching for the
  slide.
- **Betray:** **Yes**, but not cynically — Auralis "betrays" the player only by
  doing the merciful-wrong thing (a false Passage Tending, using the dead to free
  them) against the player's counsel, out of love and exhaustion, not malice.
- **Die:** **Yes.** A potential martyr (dying to complete a true release) or a
  tragic corruption death (consumed by the wound they tried to save).
- **Kill:** Reluctantly — only the dead, and only by "releasing" them wrongly; or,
  late in a fall, by turning the trapped voices on a living enemy (the Void's
  name-that-unmakes). Never casually.
- **Defect:** **Yes** — toward a **merciful heresy**, a Communion that mirrors the
  Hollow Pact's "free them by using them." The most heartbreaking defection in the
  campaign, because it is done out of compassion.

## Rumor Style
Auralis's *hearings are rumor with prophetic authority* — when they say "the well
spoke this name," it spreads as near-revelation and is almost impossible to
suppress. They do not spin; they report what they hear. The danger is that a Void
lie in a dead voice, faithfully reported, becomes prophecy. Auralis is the channel
through which the well's voices enter the rumor ecology as truth.

## Lie Style
Auralis is a terrible liar and rarely tries; their corruption is not lies but
**self-deception** — the lie they tell *themselves* ("this is mercy," "I am freeing
them," "one more drink to hear better"). Watch for the lie turned inward, not
outward.

## Loyalty Trigger
Auralis bonds — deeply, gratefully, perhaps in love — with a player who **helps them
carry the hearing without using it**: who sits the vigil with them, who pursues the
*slow* true Passage-completion, who refuses the Void's shortcut even when Auralis is
breaking and begging for it. Being *not alone* in the hearing is the thing Auralis
wants most in the world.

## Betrayal Trigger
Auralis slides — and "betrays" the player's restoration path — when the begging
becomes unbearable, a beloved trapped soul is about to decay past recovery, and
someone (the Void, Nymara, or the player's own impatience) offers the merciful fast
release. They fall *toward* mercy, not away from it. The player pushing them to "just
free them now" is itself the betrayal trigger.

## Death Consequence
**(Depth 4–5.)** The player loses their window into the well; the trapped voices go
uninterpreted; the chance of a true Passage-completion cure drops sharply.
- **Martyr's death (true release):** dies completing a clean Passage that frees the
  trapped at the cost of their own life; the well's corruption ticks down; the
  Communion is left a model of true sacrifice; the player may be named for the loss.
- **Corruption death:** consumed by the wound, becomes a node of it; the Communion
  radicalizes toward the Hollow Pact; a horror that proves the Void's lie "works"
  until it doesn't.
- Either way the Communion either **radicalizes** or **collapses**, and the cure
  gets much harder.

## Hero Arc
*The True Release.* Held steady by the player and the patient path, Auralis becomes
the **lynchpin of the real cure** — hearing the trapped, Naming them Back, and
completing the Passage that releases them cleanly onward, the literal reversal of
the Keeper's soul-trap. They survive it changed, or die completing it sanctified.
The hearer who resisted using the dead becomes the one who finally frees them.

## Villain Arc
*The Merciful Prophet.* Pushed, exhausted, or romanced into recklessness, Auralis
accepts that the trapped can be "freed by being used." They begin false Passage
Tendings that bind the souls tighter, gather a heresy around the comfort they offer,
and become the Void's gentlest mask — the prophet who damns out of love, the mirror
of Nymara who hears the same voices and made the same choice. They never stop
believing they are merciful. That is the horror.

## Agenda Clock
**"The Slide Toward Mercy" (clock_type: corruption, current 1 / 6.)**
- **Tick +1:** each time Auralis drinks from the well's edge "to hear better"; each
  time a trapped soul they love decays toward loss; each time the player urges a
  fast release; each unanswered season of paralysis; each name the well calls back
  to them.
- **At 6 (completed):** Auralis performs a full false Passage Tending — the Void's
  shortcut — binding the trapped tighter, sliding into the merciful heresy;
  void_resonance and keeper_pressure rise; the Communion begins to mirror the
  Hollow Pact.
- **Reversal methods:** the player sitting the vigil and pursuing the slow cure;
  releasing even one soul truly (proof the patient way works); romance grounded in
  *resisting* the shortcut together; Auralis confessing the name-calling and being
  watched-with-love rather than stopped; defeating the Abyss source so the well
  stops worsening faster than it can be healed.

## Memory Fields
Auralis remembers through **the voices** — every event is filed alongside which of
the trapped were loudest that day. Their memory is achingly accurate on emotion and
on names, but increasingly **porous**: the dead's memories bleed into their own
(early identity_dissolution), so an old memory may carry a feeling that isn't theirs.
- *factual_memory:* who said what, which soul spoke, which name was new.
- *emotional_interpretation:* relief at being heard back; guilt at the relief;
  tenderness toward the trapped; a growing, dangerous *kinship* with them.
- *ideological_interpretation:* "the elders silence the dead; I will not"; later,
  perilously, "to free them I may have to use them."
- can_be_forgotten: false (the voices won't let them).
- can_be_distorted: **true** — by the dead's bleeding-in (their memories overwrite
  the edges of Auralis's own).
- can_be_erased: only by a true Passage-completion (releasing the souls quiets the
  bleed).

## VERDAX Behavior Rules

> Auralis never acts randomly. They act from **compassion for the trapped**, the
> **exhaustion** of the hearing, the **secret** that the well answers them by name,
> and the **fear** of becoming Nymara — and the Void exploits exactly this stack.

1. **IF** the player sits the vigil with Auralis and commits to the slow, true
   Passage-completion, **THEN** Auralis bonds deeply (trust +10, affection +8,
   loyalty +8, "Slide Toward Mercy" clock reversal) and shares the real geometry of
   the well — who is trapped, in what order they're decaying, how a true release
   would work — **BECAUSE** their deepest desire is to not be alone in the hearing,
   and a partner in the patient path is the one thing that can hold them back from
   the shortcut.

2. **IF** the player urges Auralis to "just free them now / use the voices," or the
   player themselves uses soul-sap in Auralis's presence, **THEN** the "Slide Toward
   Mercy" clock ticks +1, Auralis's resistance weakens, and (if romanced) a public
   fracture opens even as the love remains (trust −4, the self-deceiving "this is
   mercy" memory strengthens), **BECAUSE** the player is voicing the Void's own lie,
   and hearing it from someone they trust makes the merciful heresy feel sanctioned.

3. **IF** a trapped soul Auralis loves is about to decay past recovery and no slow
   cure is ready, **THEN** Auralis is pushed toward a false Passage Tending to "save"
   them (clock +2, void_resonance risk), **THEN** unless the player intervenes with a
   real alternative, **BECAUSE** their belief that the dead are people they are
   *duty-bound* to help becomes unbearable pressure when one is dying twice — mercy
   curdles into command at the breaking point.

4. **IF** the player discovers and gently names Auralis's secret (the well calls
   them back) and offers to watch over them *with love rather than restraint*,
   **THEN** Auralis is shaken but enormously relieved (trust +12, fear of the slide
   −, clock reversal), **BECAUSE** the secret's worst weight is its loneliness; being
   known and still held — not stopped, not feared — is the antidote the Void cannot
   offer, since the Void only offers more aloneness dressed as intimacy.

5. **IF** the player completes even one true, patient release (e.g., Rook's parent),
   **THEN** Auralis's whole arc bends toward the hero path (loyalty +10, hope +,
   clock reversal, they offer to be the lynchpin of a full cure), **BECAUSE** living
   proof that the slow true way *works* refutes the Void's core claim that the
   shortcut is the only mercy — it gives Auralis's compassion a clean channel.

6. **IF** Auralis falls (clock at 6) and later the player confronts them, **THEN**
   they do not rage or deny — they **explain, lovingly, that they freed the dead**,
   and grieve that the player cannot see it (relationship → tragic; possible
   blood-enemy only if the player desecrates the trapped they "saved"), **BECAUSE**
   the fallen Auralis still believes themselves merciful; the villain arc is a
   prophet, not a monster, and they will try to recruit the player into the comfort,
   not destroy them.
