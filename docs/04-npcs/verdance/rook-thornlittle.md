# Rook Thornlittle — *The Name the Well Is Learning*

> "Mama's down there. In the well. I heard it say her name — *Maelis Thornlittle* —
> clear as the dinner bell, three nights running. So she's not gone, is she? Gone
> things don't have names anymore. So she's just... stuck. And if you could get
> stuck, you could get got out. Somebody just has to be brave enough to go down and
> get her. I'm not scared of the dark. I'm scared of leaving her in it."
> — Rook Thornlittle, age twelve, at the rim of the corrupted rootwell

---

## 1. Identity

- **Name:** Rook Thornlittle (refugee child; surname taken, not born — "Thornlittle"
  is what the grove-keepers call the orphaned young the Refuge takes in)
- **Domain:** Verdance (by refuge, not birth — an unrooted child of the border)
- **Public Role:** An orphaned refugee youth of Thornveil, roughly twelve, healed of
  the wound that should have killed them but **not yet rooted** — without a grove, a
  graftbond, a settled place, or a living parent. One of "the Sheltered," the next
  generation the whole crisis is being fought over (§21).
- **Private Role:** A **living clue and a living stake.** The corrupted well has
  begun to speak the name of Rook's dead parent — *Maelis Thornlittle* — and has
  begun, faintly, to reach for **Rook's own name.** Rook is the human face of the
  mystery (§29 Chain 2) and the prize three factions are quietly courting.

Wiry, watchful, too old in the eyes for twelve. Healed in body — the sap-healers
saved them — but grief and rootlessness left them half-finished, a seedling pulled
up before it could take. Brave in the reckless way of a child who has already lost
everything and reasons there's little left to lose.

## 2. Faction Ties (the courtship)

Rook belongs to no faction and is wanted by three (§21):

- **Sap Commons (Sylune)** — offers **shelter, healing, and a future**: a grove to
  root in, a slow safe cure for the well that might free Maelis the true way. The
  "rooted and healed" path.
- **Hollow Pact (Nymara)** — offers **agency and revenge**: "your mother isn't gone,
  she's *trapped*, and the cowards won't go get her — but we will, and we'll use the
  well's own power to do it, and you can help." The "radicalized" path. Nymara, a
  bereaved parent herself, is genuinely moved by Rook and genuinely poisonous to
  them.
- **Greenwake Communion (Auralis)** — sees Rook as **gifted**: a child the well
  *speaks to* may be a child who can *hear*, a potential communer. A path that could
  heal Rook or expose them to the trapped voices before they're strong enough.

## 3. Inner Engine (belief / desire / fear / secret / duty)

- **Belief Core:** *Mama isn't gone — she's stuck, and stuck things can be gotten
  out.* Rook has converted unbearable grief into a hope with a plan, because hope
  with a plan is survivable and grief without one is not. This belief is *partly
  true* (Maelis really is trapped, §5/§29) and *catastrophically exploitable* (the
  fast way to "get her out" is the Void's lie).
- **Desire:** To **free Maelis** — to bring their mother back up out of the well — and,
  under that, simply to be *rooted*: to belong somewhere, to stop being the
  pulled-up seedling, to have a person again.
- **Fear:** That the well will **take Rook too** — and, worse and unspoken, the
  growing suspicion that part of Rook *wants* it to, because down there is where
  Mama is. Rook fears the dark less than they fear being left out of it.
- **Secret:** Rook has been **going to the well at night** and *answering it* — saying
  "I'm here" when it speaks Maelis's name, and, once, saying their *own* name back
  into it. This is the single most dangerous thing anyone in Thornveil is doing, and
  it is the mechanism by which the well is learning Rook's name (see §10). Rook hides
  it because the grown-ups would stop them, and stopping them means leaving Mama.
- **Duty (a child's):** To not abandon their mother the way Rook feels abandoned —
  to be brave enough for both of them.

## 4. Masks

- **Public Mask:** A quiet, well-behaved, grateful refugee child — easy to overlook,
  which is how Rook prefers it. The Sheltered are supposed to be seen and not
  troubling.
- **Private Truth:** Rook is conducting a secret, doomed, desperately loving rescue
  mission at the rim of a soul-trap, answering a corrupted well in the dark, and
  sliding — name by whispered name — toward becoming the next voice in it. The
  gratitude is real; so is the danger.

## 5. Relationship Function

Rook is **emotional stakes and a living clue in one** (§21). Mechanically, Rook is
the player's most visceral way into the central mystery: the well speaks Maelis's
name (a confirmable clue pointing at the corruption's nature, §29 Chain 2), and
then begins reaching for Rook's — turning an investigation into a *rescue against a
clock.* Relationally, Rook is a ward, a younger-sibling bond, a recruiting prize,
and a potential tragic loss. The player's choices decide whether Rook is **rooted**
(healed, belonging), **radicalized** (the Pact's revenge-soldier), or **lost** (taken
into the well — the soul-flow gut-punch of §21).

## 6. Capability Flags

- **Can Romance?** No — Rook is a child; categorically not a romance candidate.
  (Relationship is ward / younger-sibling / charge to protect.)
- **Can Betray?** Not maliciously. A radicalized or desperate Rook may *deceive* the
  player to keep their night-visits secret, or run to Nymara — betrayal-as-grief, not
  treachery.
- **Can Die?** **Yes — and a death especially weighted (§21 death_impact).** If Rook
  dies *into the well,* it is a child trapped — a soul-flow catastrophe.
- **Can Kill?** No (a frightened child might lash out, but Rook is not a combatant).
- **Can Defect?** **Yes — radicalization into the Hollow Pact** if abandoned, or being
  *lost into the well* (the ultimate "defection," from the living to the trapped).
- **Can Start Rumors?** Yes, unwittingly — Rook repeating what the well "said" seeds
  prophetic-dread rumors (§24: the trapped voices as oracle).

## 7. Voice & Distortion

- **Rumor Style:** *Unwitting oracle.* Rook doesn't spread rumors on purpose — Rook
  *reports the well,* and a grieving child saying "the well told me Mama's coming
  back" or "the well said a new name last night" is repeated through the town as
  dread-prophecy (§24: "the well speaks the names of those it will TAKE"). Rook is a
  rumor *source* without meaning to be, which makes Rook a vector the Pact and the
  Keeper can amplify.
- **Lie Style:** *A child's loyal concealment.* Rook lies only to protect the
  night-visits and the rescue — "No, I haven't been to the well," "I just couldn't
  sleep." Small, transparent, easily caught lies told out of love and fear, not
  cunning. The player who catches them gently can pull the whole secret out.

## 8. Triggers

- **Loyalty Trigger (what earns Rook):** Being *taken seriously* about Maelis rather
  than soothed and dismissed — and being given a true, hopeful alternative to the
  Pact's fast revenge: "Your mother really is trapped, and we are going to free her
  the right way, and you are going to help, and you are going to be safe." Rook bonds
  hard and fast to any adult who neither lies to them nor abandons them. A player who
  helps free Maelis truly earns Rook's whole heart.
- **Betrayal Trigger (what loses Rook):** Abandonment — being left alone with the
  grief while the grown-ups "decide" (the Heartmoot's paralysis, felt by a child).
  An abandoned Rook walks straight into Nymara's open arms, because the Pact is the
  only faction that *says yes to going down after Mama.* Also: catching the player
  using corrupted sap can either horrify Rook *or* — dangerously — convince Rook that
  the fast way is the only way anyone's willing to try.

## 9. Consequence Standards

- **Death Consequence (§21 death_impact; Chunk 4 death doctrine):** If Rook dies —
  **especially into the well** — it is a **soul-flow gut-punch** (a *child* trapped,
  the worst kind of held dead), a **radicalizing event** for the whole refugee
  population ("the Refuge let its youngest be taken"), and a **damning verdict on the
  Refuge** that failed its most defenseless. The well gains a child's voice — a new,
  potent "oracle" the Hollow Pact will weaponize and sanctify. Civilian morale and
  spiritual health both crater; Ilyra may break; the player's name takes the weight
  ("the one who let the child go into the well"). Conversely, a Rook *saved* —
  Maelis released, Rook rooted — is one of the campaign's strongest galvanizing
  victories. *The dead matter here at the maximum: a trapped child is the image of
  everything Verdance exists to prevent.*
- **Hero Arc (Rooted):** *The Seedling That Took.* Taken seriously, kept safe, and
  given the true path — the player and Sylune/Auralis free Maelis through a real
  Passage-completion (§29 Chain 2 intervention) before the well claims Rook. Maelis
  is released at peace; Rook grieves *completely,* the way the cycle intends; Rook
  roots — a grove, a graftbond-to-be, a place. Possibly becomes a healthy young
  Greenwake communer, the gift realized without the wound. The future Verdance was
  fighting for, won.
- **Villain Arc (Radicalized / Lost):** *The Pact's Child or the Well's New Voice.*
  Abandoned to the paralysis, courted by Nymara, Rook concludes the only people
  willing to go down after Mama are the Hollow Pact — and joins them, becoming a
  grief-fueled recruit and, eventually, a brewer of the soul-sap that traps more
  Maelises. OR, worse, the night-visits complete: the well learns Rook's name fully,
  identity_dissolution sets in, and Rook is *lost into the well* — a living child
  become a trapped voice, the cruelest possible "reunion" with their mother.

## 10. The Well-Name Mechanic (clue + clock)

This is Rook's defining canon mechanic (§29 Chain 2; §5 identity_dissolution). It is
**both a clue the player investigates and a clock counting down toward Rook's loss.**

**As a clue:** The well speaking *Maelis Thornlittle* is *confirmable* (via Greenwake
communion or tree-memory reading) and *diagnostic* — it proves the corruption is a
**soul-trap holding the dead with their names intact** (not mere blight), pointing
the player toward the true nature of the wound and, if traced, toward the **Abyss
incision** that caused it. Each new name the well speaks is a piece of evidence.

**As a clock:** The well does not "choose" Rook at random — it is *learning* Rook's
name **because Rook keeps answering it** (the secret, §3). Every night-visit feeds
the binding. The clock below tracks the slide from *the well holds Rook's dead
parent* → *the well speaks toward Rook* → *the well speaks Rook's name* →
*identity_dissolution begins* → *Rook is lost.* The player can *stop the visits,*
*race a Naming-Back/Passage to free Maelis* (which removes Rook's reason to go), or
*trace and disrupt the Abyss cause* — any of which reverses the clock. Ignoring it
completes the binding.

## 11. Memory Fields

hole at the center. They remember Maelis in flashes (factual), file every adult by
*did they take me seriously or soothe me away* (emotional), and have built a whole
private theology around the well's voice (ideological). Rook forgets nothing about
Mama and forgives instantly anyone who promises to help get her back — which is the
danger.

## 13. VERDAX Behavior Rules (IF / THEN / BECAUSE)

1. **IF** the player takes Rook seriously about Maelis and offers a true, hopeful
   alternative to the Pact's fast revenge, **THEN** Rook bonds hard (trust/affection
   up), confides more, and can be persuaded to stop the night-visits — reversing
   `pc_well_learns_rooks_name`. **BECAUSE** Rook's belief is "stuck means save-able"
   and their desire is to free Maelis *and* be rooted; an adult who believes them
   and won't abandon them gives Rook a way to have both without giving themselves to
   the well (loyalty_trigger).

2. **IF** the player (or the Heartmoot) abandons Rook to the paralysis — soothing,
   deferring, leaving the child alone with the grief — **THEN** Rook drifts toward
   Nymara and the Hollow Pact, and the night-visits continue (clock advances).
   **BECAUSE** the Pact is the only faction that *says yes* to going down after Mama;
   to an abandoned, grieving child, agency-and-revenge beats patience-and-"next
   season" every time (§9: the bereaved are the Void's likeliest converts; §21
   radicalized-if-abandoned).

3. **IF** Rook continues answering the well at night (visits not discovered/stopped),
   **THEN** the well learns Rook's name further, `pc_well_learns_rooks_name` ticks,
   faint blight follows Rook, and identity_dissolution begins. **BECAUSE** the binding
   is *fed by Rook's answering* (§5; §29 Chain 2 — the corruption reaches the living
   through contact and consent, even a child's loving consent); the clock is a
   mechanism, not fate, and it advances only while the cause continues.

4. **IF** the player races a true Naming-Back / Passage-completion and frees Maelis
   before Rook's binding completes, **THEN** Rook is saved (rooted arc), local well
   corruption ticks down, and Ilyra/Auralis/community trust rise. **BECAUSE** freeing
   Maelis removes Rook's reason to go to the well at all, and a true patient Passage
   reverses the Keeper's soul-trap (§25 IF/THEN; §29 Chain 2 intervention) — curing
   the cause, not just the symptom.

5. **IF** the player uses corrupted sap / weaponized rot in front of Rook, **THEN**
   Rook either recoils in fear OR, dangerously, concludes the fast way is the only
   way anyone will actually try — making the Pact's pitch land harder. **BECAUSE** a
   grieving child reads every method through "will this get Mama back"; the cardinal
   violation can repel Rook or recruit Rook depending on whether anyone has offered a
   true alternative (Void exploits the desperate via the merciful-shortcut lie, §9).

6. **IF** Rook is lost into the well, **THEN** a mythic soul-flow wound forms, a new
   "Hollow oracle" (a child's voice) is created for Nymara to weaponize and sanctify,
   the refugees radicalize, and the player's name takes the weight. **BECAUSE** a
   trapped *child* is the maximal image of the Keeper's victory and the Refuge's
   failure (§21 death_impact); the dead matter at their heaviest here, and the
   rumor ecology will fight over whether it indicts the player, the elders, or the
   cycle itself (§24).

---

*Rook Thornlittle acts from belief (Mama is stuck, not gone), desire (free her; be
rooted), fear (the well taking them — and wanting it to), secret (the night-visits
and the name spoken into the dark), and a child's duty (don't abandon her the way I
feel abandoned). Rook never acts randomly: every brave, terrible choice is a child's
love doing the only math it knows.*
